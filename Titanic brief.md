I created three engineered features: FamilySize (SibSp + Parch + 1), IsAlone (whether the passenger was traveling alone), and AgeGroup (categorizing age into Child, YoungAdult, Adult, Senior, Unknown). These features help differentiate survivors and non-survivors, for example, passengers traveling alone or in small families had slightly lower survival rates, and certain age groups showed distinct survival patterns. Using classes (Passenger and TitanicDataset) allowed me to structure the data cleanly, encapsulate statistics, missing values, and feature engineering, and easily export the processed dataset to JSON for submission.
